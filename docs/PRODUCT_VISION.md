# EasyCommunity Product Vision

**Goal:** Lightweight, trusted and searchable community discovery and reference platform.

## Goal protection
The product may reuse shared Easy Platform capabilities, but unrelated product scope must not be introduced without evidence, Council review and formal approval.

## MVP scope
- Searchable verified local services and places
- Accessible-place metadata
- Verified local notices
- Near-future local impact information
- ParkEasy-supported venue discovery and deep links
- Admin evidence and recommendation workflow
- Single-hand left/right interaction mode
